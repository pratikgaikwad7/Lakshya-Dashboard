# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

LAKSHYA is used by administrators, PMO users, and plant-scoped SDC coordinators to track trainees, batches, semester evaluations, and student records across Tata Motors locations.

## Product Purpose

The product provides one controlled place to maintain and inspect student records, evaluation scores, batch participation, attrition, and population distributions. Success means authorized users can understand the current student population and move from an aggregate view to the relevant trainee record quickly.

## Positioning

LAKSHYA combines role- and plant-scoped access, maintained trainee records, semester evaluation workflows, bulk imports, and direct student drill-downs in one operational tracking system.

## Operating Context

Users review plant, batch, branch, gender, semester, status, and evaluation information; apply filters; upload student or evaluation workbooks; update semester evaluations; and inspect individual trainee histories.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a student tracking system, not a target-management or forecasting system.
- Do not invent targets, benchmarks, deadlines, risk classifications, or performance claims.
- Preserve gender distribution, plant totals, attrition by plant, active batch breakdown, diploma branch distribution, evaluation score distribution, filters, and student-record drill-downs.
- Admin and PMO users may access cross-location views. SDC coordinators remain scoped to their assigned plant.
- Existing `/user_dashboard` behavior and presentation must remain available unchanged while experimental dashboard alternatives are compared.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is LAKSHYA. The interface should remain professional, operational, and appropriate for an enterprise training environment.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains live student, batch, evaluation, attrition, plant, branch, and demographic data plus existing dashboard queries and drill-down routes. No target or benchmark dataset is available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Present current tracking information clearly without pretending to predict outcomes.
- Keep detailed data accessible while making the overview easier to scan.
- Let users move from aggregate distributions to individual trainee records.
- Respect role and plant access boundaries throughout every view.
- Prefer accurate, explainable states over decorative analytics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core tracking and filtering workflows should remain keyboard accessible, visibly focused, readable at common enterprise laptop sizes, and usable on smaller screens.
